# Narwani 2019: frozen independent forecast contract (2026-11-25 JST)
Independent aquatic experiment, not a replay of Kansas. Article DOI 10.1098/rspb.2019.1857, data DOI 10.5061/dryad.qv9s4mw99, source workbook verified Zenodo 4941856. Source metadata only inspected before this commit; no contrast outcomes or forecast scores used in selecting rules. Original study already reports cyanobacterial dominance and joint foundation-species effects. This is an exploratory cross-domain forecasting benchmark, not a new discovery of those effects or a test of causal history/competition mechanism.

Twenty ponds, four each O/C/M/D/MD. C is nutrient-pulsed with neither foundation species; M macrophytes, D mussels, MD both, same nutrient protocol; O unpulsed/no foundation. Treatment and presence fields checked; Nutrients metadata text is reversed relative to article/observed treatment labels. Use labels and presence, do not estimate time-specific pulse causal effects. One pond is one experimental unit. Allocation across spatial Block differs by arm; Rep is not paired. Use separate within-arm 2-train/2-test pond sets, all 6x6 combinations; overlapping splits and repeated weeks are NOT independent. All 20 ponds retained (including 2C, as in original study). Primary MD-C; secondary M-C,D-C,C-O. No p-value from split cells. 

Seventeen microscopy weeks, two with a missing biovolume morphotype: 7,10. Complete-case whole-week common grid, 15 dates and 300 pond-week observations. No zero imputation. Exclude six reported group totals from 66 source morphotype columns; no double count. Convert biovolume from um^3/mL to mm^3/L by 1e-6, not measured dry mass. All morphotypes include some non-algal or unresolved organisms; do not call their summed biovolume total algal biomass. Conservative external taxonomic mapping frozen in scorer (cyanobacteria, confidently classified other algal morphotypes, unresolved/other). Ambiguous/misspelled names excluded from named classes, not assigned from abundance data. Alternative two-group map cyanobacteria vs all other recorded morphotypes. Compare with independently archived six group totals (cyanobacteria vs five other classes); audit discrepancies rather than force equality. Original group totals complete at 17 times, same 15-date grid for comparability; only simple forecasts for that sensitivity.

Forecast past observations of training ponds only, first 5 complete observations as warmup. Forecast each of last 10 observations for held ponds. Irregular time spacing: previous sampled observation, not fixed-duration lag. Same previously frozen Kansas forecasting functions and penalty 10, plus penalty scaled by group count / 66, no tuning. Fit history and state ridge at morphotype and aggregate resolution, compare aggregate direct vs bottom-up on IDENTICAL targets. Zero, previous, cumulative benchmarks. All 360 dependent test cells per contrast. Score species and groups separately; never compare raw SSE across resolutions. Evaluate signed contrast persistence from whole-arm mean per week plus held-half prevalence and concurrent learned sign transfer; distinguish these from forward forecast. Report opposite direction cyan positive/other algae negative, reverse direction, neither. Ambiguous group assignment bounds by adding min/max sum of unresolved contrast to each named group, worst-case across possible reallocations. No mechanistic claim: observed group contrast can reflect seasonal dynamics, ecological sorting, shared environment or measurement. No randomized order/recruitment experiment. Preserve all nulls; no pooling with Kansas, Saine or other experiments. No primary DB/main/PR3-5 modifications in this benchmark.
