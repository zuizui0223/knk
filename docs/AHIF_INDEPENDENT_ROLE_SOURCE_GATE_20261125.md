# Independent non-grassland role persistence source gate
2026-11-25 JST. Kansas forecast frozen. Seek independently conducted manipulations of community development with repeated taxa-level abundance observations and externally fixed grouping. Separate formation-history treatment from disturbance and state prediction. No outcome scoring until schema, units and assignment verified.
1. Jiang & Patel 2008 Ecology 10.1890/07-1263.1. Simultaneous vs five sequential arrival schedules of five preassigned protist groups crossed with disturbance. Original paper already tests history x disturbance and later colonizer loss. Official publisher Figshare collection 10.6084/m9.figshare.c.3300440. Need actual original abundance matrix, not figures or reported diversity summaries; check collection metadata and files.
2. Narwani et al. 2019 Proc R Soc B 10.1098/rspb.2019.1857. Pond foundation species crossed with nutrient disturbance; original article already reports interactive effects and cyanobacterial dominance. Zenodo public record 4941856, original dataset DOI 10.5061/dryad.qv9s4mw99; workbook Narwani_et_al_2019_Proceedings_B_data.xlsx MD5 c5f52c568a1119429093d3db1603e59c. Inspect worksheet structure and metadata, no outcomes. Need repeated taxon-level biomass, same pond identity and treatments. Not arrival-order experiment.
3. Wojcik et al. nutrient pulse food-web chemostats, Zenodo 14773023, archive md5 42c1a05d90a8d328351caa6431555774 (98.7MB). 24 vessels, four algae and three rotifers, pulse timing differs across treatments; all pulsed, no unpulsed counterfactual. Inspect manifest only here; defer binary archive to a separate validated analysis if needed.
4. Hayashi et al. 2026 Ecology Letters 10.1111/ele.70388, Zenodo 19396153. Eight inoculum settings, 96 wells each, four times. Existing candidate ST_HAYASHI2026. Founder size not arrival sequence; ASV relative read proportions cannot support absolute compensation. Retrieve official metadata only. Require plate allocation audit, same-well linkage and externally defined group mapping for a future relative-response forecast.
5. Ojima & Jiang 2016 exact history×dispersal×disturbance original Dryad workbook unavailable (previous HTTP403); no workaround. Diehl et al. 2022 Ecology 10.1002/ecy.3674 is relevant initial vs spontaneous phytoplankton experiment, but Zenodo 4715500 contains model and temperature files, not original taxon profiles; Dryad 10.5061/dryad.sxksn0330 required. No mislabeling model output as observations.
