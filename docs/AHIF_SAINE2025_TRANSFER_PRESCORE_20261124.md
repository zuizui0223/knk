# Saine — cross-site, cross-year partner-identity response prediction

2026-11-24. Exploratory extension frozen before scoring. Previous source-locked analyses (446 complete logs, 347 treated, 99 sterile controls) found small binary named centroid effect at two years robust to non-target read rarefaction, but NO multiplicity-adjusted paired TV buffering effect and NO nine-way identity omnibus effect. This test asks whether finer response signatures nonetheless generalize. Same 460-log source experiment; no new independent study.

## Estimand and prediction

Use the exact original 4653 non-target OTU roster, normalized read counts and separate sorted ranks (trailing zero columns may be dropped). Signed within-log change at year 1 (2020−2019) and year 2 (2021−2019). Same original positive complete QC triplets, no outcome-dependent exclusions. Within each site x natural/felled stratum, each of the nine introduced-species group means minus the sterile control group mean yields one response vector. Equal weighting of species and of the two contexts and five sites, not raw log weighting. Shared controls and repeated time explicitly retained.

Outer leave-one-forest-site-out (five folds): training-site year-1 vectors predict the held-out site's year-2 group response. Baseline P0 = zero intervention response; P1 = mean training-site year-1 response averaged over all nine introduced species, separate for natural/felled context; P2 = P1 + alpha times centered species-specific response from training-site year 1 within context. Alpha in fixed grid {0,.25,.5,.75,1}, selected only by inner leave-one-site-out among the four outer-training sites, minimizing pooled squared prediction error for their year-2 centered species contrasts. This nested procedure uses no held-out site's post-treatment outcomes for training or selection. Equal stratum×species weights, no outcome-driven feature selection. Full-strength alpha=1 reported separately as descriptive stress test; no arbitrary tuning. Main paired loss gain = (sum outer P1 SSE − sum outer P2 SSE)/sum outer P1 SSE; positive is better. Separate named and rank metrics; neither score compares effect scale across representations. Report all five outer folds (alpha, zero/pooled/identity losses and gains), and total pooled gain over zero descriptive. The target is an experimentally derived group-average response, not individual-community prediction or a directly measured competition coefficient. Inner training uses year-2 data only at other sites; no pure prospective year prediction claim.

## Conditional null

Conditional on original complete QC cohort and introduced-vs-control membership, permute the 9 introduced identities among whole treated log trajectories within each original site x natural/felled stratum, preserving each observed arm size, holding control identities fixed. 999 permutations with shared assignment stream for named and rank; recompute group means and all nested alpha choices for EVERY draw. Upper-tail p for observed outer relative gain, plus-one correction. Holm across 2 primary tests. Null is no species-identity-specific response (conditional on binary introduction and post-treatment QC), not no species interactions or exact full-cohort ITT. Five fixed sites not independent publication replicates; no fold t-test, no general climate inference. Absence of predictive gain or non-significance is retained as a failed predictive hypothesis. No mechanism claim from a positive result; independent partner-effect assay still required.
