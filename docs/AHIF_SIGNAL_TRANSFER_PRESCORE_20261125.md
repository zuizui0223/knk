# Training-only contrast reliability predicts directional response — cross-source prescore

2026-11-25 JST. Child of PR9 head b0304517af4b462ae3a108e0cd436c20b69ea98a. Hypothesis motivated by retrospective Mancuso frequency association. This is a new retrospective cross-source diagnostic, NOT a new independent publication or formal meta-analysis. Keep all previous results/branches and ledger frozen.

Source roster: four previously source-audited experiments (pond, Kansas, POEM, field), exact 19 panels and archived replay checks from cross-source scorer; plus previously frozen Mancuso Experiment1 full-bin relative-read composition, 8 eligible panels, source hashes and author joins unchanged. Experiment2 excluded from primary due unresolved sequencing labels and depth STOP; no favorable panel selection. Kansas provenance conditional. Include all original taxa. Keep source units and paired blocks, original disjoint half splits, all t>=2 target dates as PR8. Reproduce archived per-source sign gain and same-direction fraction before using new predictors. No new significance tests from dependent split cells.

For every fold/target date t, use only TRAINING physical units at t-1 and earlier. Let d=mean treated minus mean control at t-1, Q=||d||², W=trace estimated sampling variance of contrast means (variance of matched differences/n for paired blocks, sum of two arm sample variances/n otherwise). Units squared; W includes real replicate heterogeneity, not solely observation error. S=Q-W, retain negative; U=(Q-W)/(Q+W) bounded [-1,1], undefined if Q+W=0. Scale by squared total mean abundance of both arms (T²) for cross-unit strength and variance; log1p(1000 Q/T²), log1p(1000 W/T²). The finite-sample U estimate is noisy (two or three training replicates), not calibrated biological SNR; no use of held samples, even at past times. State support, response support, sign balance, and top contributor share as baseline covariates. No observed target magnitudes or contemporaneous held values in features. Fixed models: intercept; structure (log1p nominal taxa, log1p state support, log1p response support, top absolute response fraction, absolute signed-mass imbalance); strength = structure + scaled Q; reliability = strength + U; noise = strength + scaled W (alternative, not selection). Fixed ridge lambda .1, standardize using training-only weighted mean/SD; intercept unpenalized. Predict signed-vs-signblind gain (unclipped), same-direction gross share (clip 0..1) separately. Evaluate squared error by leave-one-source-out on four absolute-measurement experiments (pin-hit/count/biomass not physically comparable; normalized prediction target only). Compare reliability vs strength as primary, also strength vs structure, noise vs strength. Equal fold weight within panel/date, then equal dates/panels/source. Grouped holdout POEM+field jointly to guard lab dependence; exclude Kansas sensitivity with three absolute sources; never infer significance or formal independent uncertainty from cells. Separate extrapolation to Mancuso1: train only the four absolute sources and test the one compositional study, with no training on microbial responses. This is measurement-lane transfer, not pooled effect; test three fixed depth-eligible panels as sensitivity (no rerarefaction required for that subset; full-bin input). Prespecify coverage, leave-source scores and descriptive source-specific correlations of U with signed gain. Negative/undefined results retained. Predictor utility does not show a causal treatment effect, temporal memory, intrinsic interaction, or identify a noise mechanism. Prior frequency association remains exploratory.
