# Independent interaction-to-structure benchmark: Ishizawa et al. 2024

2026-11-25 JST. Pre-outcome contract, after Replay/Saine results. Article PNAS 121:e2312396121, DOI 10.1073/pnas.2312396121; original public Zenodo version 8433832 (concept 8213537). Original authors measured 127 nonempty subsets of seven duckweed-associated bacterial strains, and independently observed 5–12 flasks per subset. No randomized arrival-order contrast in main factorial composition experiment. This is a distinct bottom-up compositional perturbation/response prediction lane, not history-specific causal mediation.

Original paper already shows that trio-derived interactions predict multi-strain abundances better than pairwise interactions, and discusses compensation of pairwise interactions in higher-order settings. Do not claim first demonstration of interaction-based community prediction. Novel narrower diagnostic: does independent low-order interaction information improve *identity-free rank architecture* versus named composition, and how is the change in named TV error decomposed?

Source checks: exact public MD5s for raw CFU table, published pair/trio point-prediction tables, author R scripts and functions from version 8433832. Inspect original raw header; parse strain/system/nspecies/cfu, require positive finite counts, stable membership and complete 127 subsets; exactly 7 singleton, 21 pair, 35 trio and 64 held-out 4–7-member subsets. Verify 448 distinct strain/subset cells, source replicate ranges, equal within-subset per-strain sample count (if no physical flask ID, no replicate-level covariance or inferential CI). Use log10 mean CFU for each strain/subset, matching author's reported point estimand. Note CFU per unit host mass; sum of strain geometric means is NOT mean total CFU across physical flasks. No raw source redistribution.

Prediction model A: mono only, each present strain's single-strain log CFU. B: original published Eq.4 pair model, using ONLY mono/pair measurements. C: original Eq.3 trio model, using ONLY mono/pair/trio measurements. Reconstruct C_ij^(k) as difference of mean log abundance of i between subset with j vs same subset without j, divided by mean log abundance of j in full small subset. For target S size n>k, RHS is mean log abundance of i across k-subsets containing i; diagonal 1, off-diagonal −(n−k)/(n−1) times mean C_ij across k-subsets containing i,j. Solve linear system, reject nonfinite or near-singular rather than dropping. Source author pair/trio point prediction tables must join exactly to raw cell keys and reproduce observed and predicted means within 1e−6 log units in 4–7-member targets, otherwise STOP before ecological interpretation. Source tables used only to check, not fit. No targets used for coefficient estimation or tuning. All 64 targets included, 35/21/7/1 across sizes. Four nonempty groups of target sizes are finite same-strain subset families, NOT 64 independent study replications.

Endpoints: for each target, close geometric mean CFU vectors for each observed/predicted profile, sort within same fixed seven strain roster (absent taxa zero); TV named error N=0.5*L1, sorted error R=0.5*L1, identity assignment component A=N−R (nonnegative by rearrangement). The arithmetic inequality alone is not a biological finding. Compare mono, pair, trio on the identical targets. Direct paired decomposition of pair-to-trio improvement ΔN= N_pair−N_trio = ΔR + ΔA. Sum/average signed improvements, count improved/worsened/tied targets, report by richness with equal target weights, and equal-richness-group sensitivity. Report rank and identity improvements separately (same units). No unstable division for near-zero baseline; report SSE/TV absolute means, relative gains only for positive denominators. Secondary descriptive Simpson, top1 and log10 sum-geometric-CFU errors; no claims about species richness prediction since membership is given. No p-values, pseudo independent tests, or inference to other ecosystems from complete combinatorial panel sharing strains and training subsets. No claim of biochemical interaction coefficient or independently randomized history. Report all nulls and failures.
