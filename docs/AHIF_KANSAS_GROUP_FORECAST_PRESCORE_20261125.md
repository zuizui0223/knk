Same curated Ladouceur 2020 data (DOI 10.1111/ele.13566; Figshare 10.6084/m9.figshare.12319682.v1), 120 direct mass states, 4 block labels, 100 species labels, 15 source time codes. Question: does directly forecasting aggregated experimental roles or sown functional guilds improve *the same group-level response* relative to forecasting individual species then summing? Do not compare species SSE directly to group SSE or infer the aggregation itself proves improved prediction. Primary target: 3 mutually exclusive groups (sown-list 27, other named 62, unresolved 11). Sensitivities: exact23-only roster (four synonym matches moved to unresolved), and published functional guilds of the 27 matched sown species (AF, C4-PG, PLF, PNLF; C3-PG has no observed mapped species), plus other named/unresolved. Functional classification unavailable for other named taxa; do not call these a whole-community functional-trait partition. Also collapse guild forecasts back to broad3 for identical broad target comparison. Mapping immutable, no outcome-dependent groups. 
Use identical 6 oriented 2+2 whole-block splits and expanding training ages, predict ages 6–15 (60 dependent cells). No current-year or held-block observations in predictors or fits. For each representation, compute actual held group response by sum of original species mass differences. Existing forecasts from score_ahif_kansas_forecast.py (zero, previous, cumulative, history_ridge, state_ridge, penalty10) are computed identically and archived species SSE checked for every cell/model. Bottom-up sum with fixed partition; direct group ridge trained on aggregate responses with the same previous and cumulative predictors. Direct state ridge uses lagged group control mass and share, global lagged control total/richness/Simpson from the original species profile and lag group response×global Simpson, i.e. same global reference features as species model. Train-only centering/scaling; intercept unpenalized; group seen mask from training historical aggregated states. Direct ridge penalty 10 primary, fixed per-row-scaled 10*n_groups/100 sensitivity (both fixed, report all, not select best). Direct previous and cumulative must equal species-summed equivalents to tolerance, so no redundant models. Do not fit group-specific slopes or pick penalty/year using heldout outcomes. 
Primary descriptive contrast direct history ridge vs bottom-up history ridge on identical broad3 group target SSE. Other comparisons: direct state vs bottom-up state, both vs zero, previous and cumulative; penalty-scaled variants and guild resolution reported transparently. Report overall and age-wise SSE, per-role errors and aggregate total SSE; compare sum of predictions and targets only in same units. Report explicit role-pair sign forecasting (sown >0 and other <0), TP/FP/TN/FN, balanced accuracy if both classes, and exact signed-role matches per broad representation. This is future-sign forecasting, not the earlier held-response conditional gate. Zero predicts no opposition. No p-values, CIs, pseudo-replicates or proof of generalization; 4 blocks, same selected experiment, age codes repeated, six overlapping splits. No inference of role-specific mechanism, superiority to arbitrary grouping or actual origin of individuals. Original sowing and non-sowing groups differ by intervention membership, not measured functional similarity. No new independent effect row. Archive all outputs, nulls and checks.
