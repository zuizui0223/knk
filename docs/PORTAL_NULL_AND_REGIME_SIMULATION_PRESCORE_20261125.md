# Synthetic variance-aware forecast check — prescore 2026-11-25

Independent artificial diagnostic; not a Portal/BioTIME result. 4,000 synthetic communities per case, 30 taxa, 16 periods, two halves of five plots. Fixed seed 2026112502, lognormal mean exp(N(1.5,1.4)), independent conditional gamma-Poisson observations (species-specific alpha=exp(N(-0.5,1.5)) or Poisson). Cases: stationary Poisson; stationary heterogeneous overdispersion; stationary latent lognormal temporal rate variation (species fixed log-volatility exp(N(-1.4,.6)), subtract .5 sigma² on log scale); changed volatility regime (independent new per-species volatility in last four periods, same throughout last four, no shared direction). All are synthetic independent ecological units, no causal interaction. Fixed independent training half, held half, older 12 periods, last four disjoint lag/target pairs, forecasts as Portal M2,V,H,L,AH,VH,HH. H_oldpair_mix average of losses from mixing H with each of six older-pair vectors. Report mean paired TV improvement and MC 95% interval (simulation only), including frequency of wins and null scenarios. Negative results retained. No calibration to real counts, no claim these scenarios are exhaustive, no tuning on any observed outcome. Test whether recent response beats longer-term historical response information in synthetic stationary vs changing-volatility regimes; even a positive result does not identify interaction or assembly causality.
