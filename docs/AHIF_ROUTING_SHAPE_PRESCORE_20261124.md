## Question A: does rank response require more than a simple richness/concentration representation?

For each plot and year separately, close the fixed 58 non-target hit vector (STOP at zero total). Let k be detected richness and C=sum(p^2) Simpson concentration. Construct a k-positive geometric rank curve p_j ∝ q^j for j=0,...,k-1, q in [0,1], choosing q by bisection to match C, padding remaining ranks with zeros. For k=1 use [1,0,...]. Residual = observed sorted p minus this fitted shape. No treatment labels enter the transformation. Secondary alternative: one dominant proportion a=(1+sqrt((k-1)(kC-1)))/k and equal remaining proportions; exactly matches richness and C, generally different curve. The curves are deterministic mathematical descriptions, not generative models of communities or causal mediator adjustments.

Compute within-plot residual vector changes 2024 minus 2021. Euclidean distance, E=R2 minus median of 9,999 within-block assignment null R2 values, same original whole-plot assignments within a site across all endpoints. Primary two site geometric residual tests Holm across 2. Secondary two alternative curve residual tests Holm across 2. Also report descriptive mean squared reconstruction residual by site/year, actual original rank-change score under same permutations, and k and C range; no tests selected by significance. If residual effect not detected, cannot establish equality or that all rank history is explained; if detected, history is present in deviations from this specific curve at matched k/C, not causal mediation or proof that all richness/evenness models fail. The residual is a derived outcome using post-treatment features and may preserve sampling effects.

## Question B: direct fixed-site contrast rather than contrasting significance

Four source schedules are the complete 2x2 combination of fast/slow guild early or late (FtS=fast early/slow late, StF=fast late/slow early, SFearly=both early, SFlate=both late). Keep physical blocks intact. For each of five complete blocks at each site, use the same three orthogonal contrasts with coefficients +/-1/2: fast calendar timing, slow calendar timing, interaction of schedules. For each non-target outcome (rank change vector, richness change, Simpson concentration change), stack the three contrasts in a vector. Directly estimate the difference between fixed-site mean block contrast vectors, Euclidean norm and squared norm minus within-site sampling trace corrections. Do not compare sizes across endpoint units. Under independent approximately symmetric block-level contrast errors, use centered site-wise Rademacher wild bootstrap (HC2 residual rescaling, enumerate 2^10 signs, plus-one finite-grid correction) for exploratory equality of mean contrast vectors. This is NOT an exact randomized treatment test and sites were not randomized. Studentized statistic squared norm / trace of estimated variance of mean difference, recompute variance for each bootstrap draw; if denominator zero, explicit STOP rather than p-value. Holm across 3 approximate tests. Report raw contrast means for scalar outcomes, uncertainty caveat (5 blocks/site), plus Welch approximate site contrasts of the pre-existing FtS-StF scalar responses (richness and Simpson, separate two-test Holm family). A p-value from this approximation is a lead, not robust site moderation evidence; absence of significance is not equality. Do not promote regional/climatic causation. No assumption of exchangeability of blocks between sites. Plot-level rank residual assignment tests are distinct from model-based site tests.
