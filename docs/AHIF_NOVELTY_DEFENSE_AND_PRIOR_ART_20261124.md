# AHIF-DB — novelty defense against nearest established literature

**Date: 2026-11-24. Status: preliminary literature screening, not completed PRISMA systematic review, not confirmation that a globally unique research gap exists.** This is an original *curation protocol* built for KNK. Keep frozen v28–v38 and existing Ecology Letters manuscript untouched.

## What is definitively NOT novel

- That arrival time matters to species interactions: already a fundamental priority-effect result.
- That early arrival operates through niche pre-emption and modification: Fukami (2015), DOI https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-ecolsys-110411-160340.
- That competitive traits of both early and late plants predict priority effects: van Steijn et al. (2025), DOI https://doi.org/10.1002/ecy.4528.
- That priority effects vary across environments, species and temporal scales: Zou & Rudolf (2023), DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tree.2023.08.001; Stroud et al. (2024), DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tree.2024.02.004.
- That history-dependent community trajectories can be framed as assembly graphs: *Untangling the complexity of priority effects in multispecies communities* (2021), DOI https://doi.org/10.1111/ele.13870.
- That arrival × top-down/bottom-up interactions can modify both composition and decomposition: Leopold et al. (2017), DOI https://doi.org/10.1111/ele.12803.
- That interventions on root contact, arrival order, and removal can alter experimental priority effects: Yan et al. (2024), DOI https://doi.org/10.1186/s13717-024-00483-y.

Do not cite the absence of these details from AHIF-DB v0.1 as evidence they have not been published. These are **positive prior art novelty challenges**.

## The actual proposed scientific distinction

### Claim N1 — measurement crosswalk, not a novel ecological law
A normalized original, source-linked research dataset that classifies *experimental history intervention* × *interaction endpoint and identification level* × *named species abundance / within-guild ranks / identity-free rank geometry* × *functional/process outcomes*, preserving experimental biological units, design/age controls, missingness, effect uncertainty, publication/lab dependence and whether the same experiment measured both channels.

**What this can support after full screening:** an evidence gap table and reproducible study eligibility. It does NOT establish biological causation or unique originality by itself.

### Claim N2 — ecological hypothesis, not yet demonstrated
Experimental history-dependent changes in partner-specific net neighbor effects may predict **whether the community records history in species identity versus identity-free abundance architecture**, conditional on arrival regime, resource availability, baseline competitor asymmetry, and community richness.

**Required experiment:** Same randomized multi-species panel has (a) arrival-order intervention, (b) resident-present/absent or soil-conditioning/barrier counterfactual, (c) age-matched absolute per-species biomass and whole-community unit-level species matrix, (d) named and sorted rank structural scores with identical assignment null, and (e) out-of-sample community-level mediation/prediction rather than post hoc abundance correlations.

**Falsifier:** Under adequate power and independently randomized mechanisms, measured history-dependent net partner effects provide **no added held-out prediction** of identity-vs-rank storage differences beyond initial size, exposure duration, and species traits; or identity-vs-rank differences disappear under design-respecting nulls.

### Claim N3 — conditional channels and timescales, not universal taxonomy
Even among plants, different formation cohorts and observation grains may show distinct storage channels (POEM). And soil-conditioned solutions may modify root exploration without proportionate biomass output (Delory), a known effect requiring source author filter/exclusion sensitivity. These imply a **testable context model** rather than fungal-vs-plant taxon generalization.

**Falsifier:** Independent same-grain multiple ecosystems fail to reproduce conditional channel changes after controlling abundance geometry and selection of transformations.

## Strongest added novelty threats (independently checked against original article text)

1. **Fukami et al. (2010), Ecology Letters, DOI [10.1111/j.1461-0248.2010.01465.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1461-0248.2010.01465.x)**. Manipulated initial introduction order of 10 fungi (7 actual first-arriver histories), 3 nitrogen levels and two harvest times. The original paper already demonstrated an experimental chain **arrival history → species interactions/composition → wood decomposition/carbon release**. Therefore *a mechanism-to-community-function causal chain* alone is **NOT** novel to KNK. Its original paper reports species composition and outcomes, but we have NOT source-scored identity-free sorted abundance-rank geometry or mechanistic mediation in an original 10-fungi source matrix. The source's supplementary material is figures/species lists; quantitative raw source linkage still must be screened.

2. **Uricchio et al. (2019), American Naturalist, DOI [10.1086/701434](https://doi.org/10.1086/701434)**. The study measured 5-species competition, parameterized a multi-species population model and predicted real grassland community composition and priority effects. It reported strong exotic single-species dominance in 80% of model scenarios. Thus *interspecific competition predicts present-day community composition* is **already proven/established**; KNK must focus on **history-specific changes in empirically measured interaction effects predicting the split between named identity and identity-free rank architecture, in held-out communities**. This exact dataset appeared as **ZOU2023_R0224** in the original review corpus (Beverton–Holt-with-infection model coding). The original model/empirical source was not re-fitted in AHIF.

3. **Song, Fukami & Saavedra (2021), Ecology Letters, DOI [10.1111/ele.13870](https://doi.org/10.1111/ele.13870)**. Graph theory already describes priority-driven transient paths, cycles, alternative stable states and community assembly predictability, tested on empirical algal/ciliate assembly graphs. Crucially, its discussion **explicitly proposes** comparing experimental assembly graphs with graphs inferred from fixed pairwise interaction strengths to infer history dependence of interaction coefficients. Therefore even the *concept* of relating formed interactions and assembly outcomes is NOT novel. KNK must offer a **quantitative, empirically validated interaction-to-representation prediction** beyond that existing proposal.

4. **Zou & Rudolf (2026), Ecology, DOI [10.1002/ecy.70454](https://doi.org/10.1002/ecy.70454)**. Two *Tribolium* competitors: randomized arrival order and generation count, fitted phenomenological competition coefficients which **changed with arrival time**; overlapping generations buffered these priority effects. Thus *showing that competitive coefficients form/change with arrival order* is **already experimentally demonstrated**. AHIF `ST_GENERATIONS2026` is now **original-source audited and scored**: 414 vials and 42 original fitted coefficient summaries, with nine 2-species label-versus-top1 comparisons; see [source-authentic results](AHIF_ZOU2026_414_VIAL_RESULTS_20261124.md). These coefficient summaries were fitted on the same counted adults, not an independent mediation test. The authors' actual data/code are available at [Figshare](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.32118556.v2). Any KNK causal mechanism claim should be tested against this source.

**Practical revised novelty statement:** Do NOT claim (i) priority effects, (ii) history-dependent interaction coefficients, (iii) interaction-driven changes in whole-community composition/function, or (iv) the interaction-to-assembly-graph idea as discoveries. The narrower proposed contribution is a cross-experiment, reproducible metric and prediction: whether **independently perturbed history-conditioned biotic interaction effects** predict **identity-rich versus rank-architecture-rich historical signals** in *the same* multi-species units, across systems and holdouts. Even this is a **hypothesis until systematic source screening and causal same-panel experiments**, not an established universal law.

## Added source-backed original research test (2026-11-24)

The author-released `Zou2026Ecology` source was verified against immutable blob hashes: 414 vials, 378 mixed, 36 single, 42 fitted alpha summaries. Nine complete symmetric opposite-arrival tests (3 generations ×3 lead intervals) reveal that order-dependent species identity changes can be substantially larger than changes in identity-free two-species top1 dominance, **but top1 also changes significantly in multiple conditions**. For the 3-generation 12-day contrast, T_share difference =+.8514 and unlabeled top1 difference =+.1294; both conditional exact 400-assignment p=.005 (unadjusted across 18 tests). The fitted-alpha asymmetry vs own treatment cell T share r=.8576 is **same-data** and not external validation. All source-group findings are exploratory and not a claim of newly discovered competitive priority effects. See [full study source audit](AHIF_ZOU2026_414_VIAL_RESULTS_20261124.md).

The database also adds Fukami 2010 and Uricchio 2019 as **paper-only** prior-art entries and a six-paper DOI-checked novelty-challenge table, two papers of which have raw-source audit. No full independent 21-paper screening is claimed.
