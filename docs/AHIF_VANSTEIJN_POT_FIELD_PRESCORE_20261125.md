2026-11-25 JST. Exploratory pre-score specification after earlier independent within-paper analyses. No target cross-study results seen. ## Target and predictor

Species intersection based on exact binomials in original source and both paper Table 1 lists: 6 fast (Agrostis capillaris, Cardamine pratensis, Dianthus deltoides, Festuca rubra, Leucanthemum vulgare, Sedum acre), 4 slow (Antennaria dioica, Briza media, Phalaris arundinacea, Poa pratensis). Match exact original species names, no fuzzy mapping. Use only matched simultaneous and pre-established neighbor pot rows, focal age 10 weeks. Solo mean total biomass from 74 source pots; 206 directed rows in each paired treatment, 202 matched. Csim=(sim−solo)/(sim+solo); Cpre=(late−solo)/(late+solo); history feature D=Cpre−Csim, equal mean across measured opposite germination group neighbors in shared species set. Each focal requires at least half opposite group and >=2 matched neighbors; missing pairs explicitly report, no imputation. Also retain mean Csim and mean Cpre and solo biomass. One pot per directed species pair: noisy, not a fitted dynamic alpha or isolated niche mechanism. No unmeasured 10 field species or 58 nonsown species imputed.

## Field contrast

Use published Transect filter, all 40 persistent plots, 5 blocks at each of MAL and UME, 2021–24. Within block compare same focal late sowing date: fast focal StF−SFlate; slow focal FtS−SFlate. The other germination group is sown early instead of late, so both focal calendar dates fixed. SFlate shared across groups. Primary 2024 difference in log(1+pinpoint hits) averaged across five blocks; true absence = 0 after source verified roster. Hits not biomass or independent individuals; no abundance normalization across common subset. Raw hit differences and 2021–23 log outcomes descriptive sensitivities. No use of simultaneous-early control as proxy for same-date comparison. Prior pot D sign is expected to be positively associated with field contrast, but coefficient calibrated in field training only.

## Prediction

For each held site and held focal species, fit ridge on other site's other nine species' mean contrasts, predict held-site focal. Known pot measurements for held species allowed; this holds out field focal response and site, not entire taxon from pot assay. Ridge slope penalty 2, training-only column centering/scaling, unpenalized intercept. M0 uses field germination speed, high/low seed density, grass/forb, log(1+solo mean pot biomass), mean simultaneous Csim (all known prior to field endpoint). M1 adds pot D. Separate fixed descriptive Mtrait (speed/density/form/solo; no sim), and Mpre (M0+mean Cpre; algebraically related to D, sensitivity only). Every site/species weighted equally (20 prediction cells, not 20 independent experiments). Aggregate loss SSE, incremental gain (SSE0−SSE1)/SSE0, site and guild components, full predictions and training-slope signs. Direct primary joint statistic signed gain. 1999 shared within-block permutations of four field assignments independently across 10 original blocks, preserving same permutation across species (and across years if computed). Recompute all responses and field calibration for each draw. Upper tail plus-one reference for sharp NO SCHEDULE EFFECT; not a test of no added predictor under arbitrary nonzero schedule effects. Descriptive leave-one-species-out-of-aggregate and per-site gains; no test treating species as independently sampled. Predefine one primary endpoint/test. A positive score is predictive lead, NOT a causal bridge; two field sites and ten common species only, no causal transport, no direct mechanism mediation. No pooling with microbial prediction or rank evidence. Stop on original source checks/joins or inadequate matched partner coverage, archive nulls and failures. No raw source redistribution.
