# Lear 2024 — resident-response cancellation on unclosed counts

2026-11-25 JST. Exploratory contract before computing endpoint contrasts (public source preview already inspected, not a blinded preregistration). Article Ecology Letters 27:e14493 DOI 10.1111/ele.14493; original Zenodo v3 DOI 10.5281/zenodo.12528023. Distinct experiment from other AHIF sources. Author paper already establishes effects of event order on resident diversity and species losses. This reanalysis asks whether opposing *resident species count responses* cancel in aggregate. It is not an assay of independently measured pairwise interaction coefficients. No optimization of newcomer performance, growth or invasion conditions. Only the five resident count columns analyzed.

Source: original pinned three files (endpoint table, README, analysis Rmd); verify byte MD5s. Endpoint table 33 treatment cells planned n=6 each, 197 observed rows, documented loss in 8.1 B. Exact keys day (5,6,7,7.9,8.1,9,10,11,NI), propagule (A–D or NI), rep. Unique complete resident count observations; nonnegative integers and positive resident sum. Preview shows two repeated rep labels in day 6 A/B; no attempt to invent new IDs or pair rows on rep. Report duplicated labels and missing labels. Comparisons involving ambiguous rep cells are descriptive only and primary contrast excludes them. No row deletions or correcting identity by guessed labels. Source authors treat replicates as separate microcosms; independent physical unit assumption for unique-label groups not independently reverified. No exact randomization claim (assignment details not established).

Primary contrast: 8.1 minus 7.9 in equal-weight average over the four original propagule levels, using independent group means for each resident column. For each level d, vector Delta_d=after−before. Pooled Delta=mean_d Delta_d. Measure gross species response G=sum(abs(Delta_i)), net total response N=abs(sum(Delta_i)), cancellation C=G−N. Raw observed counts on common scale, no closure, no pseudocount; opposite-signed changes can cancel but C>=0 mechanically. C/G when G>0 else null. Also report same metrics per level and mean of within-level C; pooling across levels can additionally cancel effects and must not be interpreted as within-community compensation. Separate group responses, not paired replicates. Secondary descriptive time contrasts 9−7,10−6,11−5; latter two unique-label contrasts can have approximate intervals, 10−6 explicitly descriptive only. Retain controls separately as reference summaries, not another independent test or a source of independent per-level reference. No newcomer count outcome or growth rate analysis.

Evidence beyond arithmetic: (i) equal-level-weighted Welch/Satterthwaite intervals for each of five resident signed responses on count scale, two-sided simultaneous 95% Bonferroni across five in primary, ten in secondary unique-label pair comparisons (9−7 and 11−5). Variance=sum group sample variance/n times squared weights; finite-sample approximate, not exact design inference. Only if both a positive and a negative species response have intervals excluding zero can we claim simultaneous evidence for opposing effects for that contrast. No inference on 10−6. (ii) cross-fitted signed cancellation: 256 seeded stratified half splits within each day×level cell; for both directions, learn species signs and total sign on training halves, evaluate signed gross minus signed net on complementary held-out halves. Average two orientations and all splits (repeated splits not independent). Do separately for pooled and within-level responses. A positive cross-fit value is descriptive reproducibility of opposing signed responses, not a p-value or a confidence interval, can be negative. No split filtering except source gate; do not tune split seed. No crossfit for duplicate-label comparison. Raw mean counts and resident totals are CFU observations, not biomass or viable absolute population estimates beyond common sampling scale; zeros are non-detections, not proof of extinction. No causal compensation/interaction mechanism inferred from aggregate arithmetic. No pathogen outcome analysis, no culture methods. All resident outcomes and failures archived; no main or existing 161-effect ledger change until source audit.
