# Independent experimental transfer gate — Mancuso et al. 2021

Source: Mancuso CP et al. Environmental fluctuations reshape an unexpected diversity-disturbance relationship in a microbial community. eLife 10:e67175. DOI https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.67175. Author-published Figure 3 source data version 2: https://cdn.elifesciences.org/articles/67175/elife-67175-fig3-data1-v2.csv (population size/diversity) and https://cdn.elifesciences.org/articles/67175/elife-67175-fig3-data2-v2.csv (genus composition). Original sequencing accession PRJNA719465. Author article CC BY 4.0; retain provenance. No redistribution of source data. Previous PR8 frozen.

## Design-only screening
64 soil-derived bacterial cultures in DDR64, four disturbance intensities crossed with four frequency regimes; daily sampling over six days. Constant dilution at identical mean rate is the matched reference, NOT an untreated/no-disturbance group. Distinct washout experiment excluded, and author contamination/execution exclusions respected. Need individual bioreactor IDs, treatment rates/frequencies, sample dates and at least 4 cultures per arm with >=3 complete common dates. If only plotted group means, no unique matching IDs, incompatible source grain, or too few balanced replicates, STOP; do not pretend genus rows or sequencing subsamples are independent cultures. Source files may be compositional; report relative-abundance genus lane separately from previous absolute-unit studies, no absolute magnitude/cancellation claim. OD may be analyzed only as explicitly calibrated proxy after separate pre-score audit, not converted to true cell abundances.

## Frozen diagnostic conditional on gate
All eligible matched mean-rate constant-vs-fluctuating contrasts, no favorable selection. Fixed 2+2 splits of four cultures per arm (all disjoint choices, independent across arms), same split across dates; shared reference folds not independent. Complete case on source unit/time, no interpolation; zero-fill absent genera only if source complete-profile semantics verified. Whole-genus roster, normalized absolute arm-mean contrast target at next date; matched state, sqrt state, contrast variance, older response base and equal-weight recent response mixture as PR8. Same absolute predicted mass with directional vs sign-blind representation; report sign-weighted share of held gross compositional contrast and sign gain. Recency test (>=4 dates) uses same common historical base excluding candidate dates. Summarize equal contrasts within source, no p-values from split cells. No biological interaction or history causality from predictability. Single new publication independent of prior four, if eligible, but not meta-analysis or proof of generalization. Null and STOP outcomes kept.

This first commit only performs source metadata inspection and file hashes, not outcome scoring. Lock the actual source hashes, column mappings and treatment labels after audit and before evaluation. No change to prior source ledgers or manuscript.
