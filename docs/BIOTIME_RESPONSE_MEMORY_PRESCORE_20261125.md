## Fixed pilot
- Count-only studies (unambiguous ABUNDANCE_TYPE Count); exclude explicitly eBird-labelled source metadata, flag avian source audit before publication. Require species-level validated names, finite nonnegative counts, coordinates, known month, event ID, year; no mixing biomass/cover/count. Non-detection is zero within observed event, not extinction. Zero-total/unobserved events cannot be recovered from long table. All-taxon raw record coverage and exclusions reported. 
- Primary repeated exact lat/lon/depth stratum within study and calendar month; fixed plot identifier included if present and nonblank. Secondary equal-area approximate 100 km grid by month/depth omitted, labelled spatial-mix sensitivity. Every sample event must map to exactly one site/time. At least 4 distinct events per stratum-year, seeded split of exactly 4 into two independent 2-event means (disjoint IDs, not verified physical independence); same stratum and month for all four consecutive years. One nonoverlapping four-year window at a time per stratum (greedy earliest). No interpolation, no selecting by outcomes. Report coverage by study and realm; not representative of all 708.
- For t-3,t-2,t-1,t, predictors from split A through t-1; target distribution q_i=abs(B_i,t - B_i,t-1)/sum abs(delta B). If target gross zero, undefined; record separately. Future species unknown to predictors receive zero from both; union support retained, no renormalization over only resident taxa. Prediction baselines: most recent A abundance (M0), mean A abundance over all three known years (M1), sqrt of mean A abundance (M2; sampling noise scale); lag response p=normalized abs(A_t-2 - A_t-3), and equal-weight mixtures of p with M1 or M2 (M3/M4). If no previous response, mixture falls back to respective baseline. No fitted hyperparameters. Nonoverlapping time intervals and sample halves avoid common endpoint noise. Score total variation, top1 overlap with ties fractionally, fraction of target gross attributable to largest currently abundant taxon; log counts not used. Predefined principal comparison M4 vs M2, with M3 vs M1 as sensitivity. Study-equal mean differences and percentile bootstrap over studies; no independence claim for windows/splits. Require >=10 studies for descriptive interval, >=3 realm groups for cross-realm conclusion; report coverage and leave-one-study-out influence. Results exploratory and not direct experimental replication.
- Raw cancellation C=1-|sum(delta)|/sum|delta| using split B (only absolute counts; not proportions). Independent split A signs projected onto B and B signs onto A, signed gross and signed net as crossfit diagnostics, not unbiased C. Also report named and sorted relative-count TV and algebraic named-minus-rank (nonnegative by rearrangement; not evidence of ecological buffering by itself).
- Repeat with exchanged halves as sensitivity if implemented; no postscore tuning, no causal interaction/formation-history claim. Required follow-ups: common station roster for grid, season/method/effort auditing, observation-only null sim with heterogeneous rates, original study independence clusters, and prior-art screen before publication. This pilot only defines whether large-scale transfer is plausible.
